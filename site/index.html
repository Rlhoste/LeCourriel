A calmer way to do email

Let the assistant do the reading.
You do what matters.

Le Courriel puts an AI assistant between you and the flood. Every new message is summarised, ranked, filed, and checked for commitments—without replacing the email account you already use.

01 / Morning overview

Start with decisions,
not a message count.

Urgent mail, deadlines, tasks, meetings, and the week ahead are gathered into one quiet briefing.

  • One-line AI summaries
  • Priority and threat detection
  • Deadlines extracted automatically
02 / Tasks

A to-do list that maintains itself.

Requests become linked tasks. Replies can close them. Projects keep the work organised.

03 / Search

Ask your inbox a real question.

Search across threads in natural language and get an answer with the source messages attached.

04 / Writing partner

From rough thought
to ready-to-send.

Correct, translate, rewrite formally, or draft a complete answer from the message you are replying to.

Your signature and quoted messages stay untouched.
